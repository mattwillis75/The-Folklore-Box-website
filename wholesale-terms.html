Wholesale Terms & Conditions

We currently only sell wholesale to England, Scotland, Northern Ireland and Wales. If you are outside this region then please contact us on wholesale@thefolklorebox.com to discuss this further.

Our MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY is £75.

Accepted payments are credit and debit cards and Paypal.

Shipping can take up to 10 days. Tracking numbers will be provided.

Our couriers will try to deliver twice. It is your responsibility to ensure that goods are received, or alternative delivery times or points are organised directly with the courier, through their tracking system. Items returned to us can be recouriered to you for an additional £10 cost.

Returns: You have a 7 day window in which to notify us of items you wish to return. We only accept returns on damaged or defective items, this is because we make these items in our studio just for you. Postal cost for returns will be paid by the buyer initially. Once the items are received and deemed to be defective or damaged a refund will be given for the items and 50% of the return postal cost will be returned also. If you wish to have the items replaced, we will pay the postage.

For items lost in the post. We would ask that you deal directly with the courier for the first 7 days after the item is due. After which, please contact us and we will raise a lost parcel enquiry with the courier. Once this is fully resolved and repaid by the courier, we will issue either a refund or replacement goods.

The MINIMUM ADVERTISED PRICE for all goods will be displayed on your invoice. This is also the RRP. The wholesale order price is 60% of RRP. Our retail website thefolklorebox.com sells at this price. This price should never be undercut. General site wide RRP increases occur in the fiscal new year.

Any wholesale purchase is made subject to these terms and conditions.

Please do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions.
