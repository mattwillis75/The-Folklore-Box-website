Text:
On the first weekend in August, my folklore friends and I set out in the blazing sun for the Suffolk town of Bungay to celebrate the annaiversary of the devil dog of East Agngia - Black Shuck.

It was on August 4th 1577, amid a thuderous storm that the hellish hound burst into St Mary's Church in Bungay, wrung the necks of two worshippers before jumping to the rafters and making his exit, only to repeat the performance later in the day at Blythburgh church.

We celebrated this famous dogs murderous spree in the form of a procession. We bought along our puppets which had been made for the festival last year. They formed a line up of Norfolk's best know folklore fiends - The Lantern Man, Jack Valentine (more sweetheart than fiend really...) and the Sheringham Mermaind (also more of a music lover than a fiend...)

We walked them through the middle of Bungay before stopping in a nearby field so people could take the opportunity to get a photograph or two. After that it was home for me as I had to prepare for the Norfolk Folklore Society talks the following day. I was manning my Folklore Box shop and helping to sell books by the various speakers.

I had a great time with my fellow stallholders, East Anglian Kage and Eddy, Dominic Chorney and the Hidden Dragons Guys.  Unfortunately I was in the foyer all day so didnt manage to catch any of the talks, but I still had a great day.
