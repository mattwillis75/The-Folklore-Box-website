Description: This is a T-Shirt featuring a leaping Black Shuck - The Devil Dog of Bungay.

Black Shuck is a legendary ghostly black dog from English folklore said to roam the coastline and countryside of East Anglia. The creature is typically described as an immense, shaggy hound with glowing fiery eyes and is viewed as an omen of death or misfortune. Its name likely comes from the Old English word scucca, meaning devil or fiend.

During a violent thunderstorm,on August 4, 1577, the beast allegedly burst through the doors of St Mary's Church, struck down members of the congregation, and killed two people. Scorched claw marks left on the church door are still pointed to today as the "devil's fingerprints".

On the exact same day, about 13 miles away at Holy Trinity Church in Blythburgh, the spectral hound reportedly burst inside, killed a man and a boy, and caused the steeple to collapse through the roof, leaving matching scorch marks on the north door.

The T-Shirt is made from 100% Organic ringspun combed cotton.
