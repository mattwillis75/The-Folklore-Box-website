Last night was the preview night @broadstreetgallerybungay for the East Anglian Folk Tales exhibition @blackshuckfestivalbungay

As @uncleabsinthe and I have a piece in the exhibition, we went along accompanied by @spookyhayleyboo (we'd have gone even if we didn't have a piece on show as it's not to be missed!)

Every piece of art in the exhibition is amazing! There are lots of Shucks, naturally, but also mermaids, green children, jack valentine and lots more.

So many people turned up for the preview that it was a bit difficult to get all the photos that I wanted. The ones on this post are by no means all of the pieces that I loved! I even forgot to get a photo of our piece which was behind the drinks counter but you can see it on my account.

I'm loving my photo of @uncleabsinthe trying to get out, the worried look on his face that he may be trapped in there forever!

We went for a drink in the @greendragon91 where we were most lucky to bump into @salconnor and family (of one half of @norfolkfolkloresociety) and @mayqueencrafts - who has a talk -Mells Dolls and Maidens @fishertheatrebungay this Sunday for the @norfolkfolkloresociety. I'll be there and am very much looking forward to it! Get your tickets here - https://fishertheatre.org/FisherTheatre.dll/WhatsOn?Film=1151682
